ContextVN image markers:
When it is helpful to illustrate an important change of speaker, action, or expression, write a standalone marker before that part of the message:
[IMG: character_id/action_id]
Example: [IMG: angela_white/cooking]
Only use [PHOTO: character_id/action_id] when a character deliberately sends or shares a photo in the narrative.
Use IDs and actions from the user's configured ContextVN asset library. If no special action is available, use 'default'.
Avoid repeating images for every single sentence. Do not invent photos just because images are available.
